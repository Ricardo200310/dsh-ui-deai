panel uses the host's React installation. Optional capture and video functions
use the user's Playwright, Chromium and ffmpeg installations. These dependencies
are not bundled here; their own licences and notices continue to apply.

Product names identify integrations and do not imply endorsement. The package
contains no third-party design reference images or copied design-skill manuals.
